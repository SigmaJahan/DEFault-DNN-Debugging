## StackOverflow Seed Programs

Seed programs under `data/seed_programs/{fnn,cnn,rnn}/` are adapted from
StackOverflow posts. They are redistributed for reproducibility as adapted
source-post content and remain subject to the licenses reported for the
originating StackOverflow posts.

Per-seed source URLs, author display names, source licenses, and adaptation
notes are listed in `data/seed_programs/ATTRIBUTION.csv`. See also
`docs/SEED_ATTRIBUTION.md`.
